Relic completion across all stages
Full material list — every stage, i80 to i135

Complete ARR Zodiac Weapon relic requirements for the original A Realm Reborn relic line, stage by stage. Quantities below are per weapon (each job you level the relic for needs its own full set). Nexus onward is quest/light-gated rather than a simple item turn-in — see each stage's tip for the real requirement.

1. Relic (i80)

Job quest chain through Gerolt: trial, dungeon, beastmen, and four Hard-mode primals unsynced.

Item Qty needed How to obtain Exchange cost
Base Relic Weapon 1 Start with 'The Weaponsmith of Legend' (Vesper Bay) into 'A Relic Reborn' (Gerolt, North Shroud). Collect the Timeworn weapon, deliver a materia-melded weapon, clear the Chimera trial, run Amdapor Keep, defeat 24 beastmen, then clear Hydra + Ifrit/Garuda/Titan (Hard) unsynced. Quest Complete

2. Zenith (i90)

Trade 3 Thavnairian Mist at the Furnace to reforge your relic.

Item Qty needed How to obtain Exchange cost
Thavnairian Mist 3 With your base relic equipped, speak to Gerolt, then trade 3 Thavnairian Mist (bought with Poetics) at the Furnace to reforge into the Zenith weapon. Poetics Vendor

5. Novus (i110)

Meld 75 Alexandrite and 75 materia into a Sphere Scroll for another cosmetic upgrade.

Item Qty needed How to obtain Exchange cost
Alexandrite 75 Random drops from Gold-rated FATEs (Animus weapon equipped), Allied Seals (50 per unit), or Mysterious Maps (5 Alexandrite each, bought with Poetics). 75 Poetics
Materia (any grade) 75 Melded onto the weapon as part of the Sphere Scroll infusion. Since the Novus weapon is purely cosmetic at this point, cheap low-grade materia works fine. Market Board / Melding
Superior Enchanted Ink 3 Quest: 'Celestial Radiance' — speak to Jalzahn in Hyrstmill with Animus equipped. Buy the ink, then return with Animus unequipped to receive the Novus weapon. 25 Poetics

6. Nexus (i115)

Farm 2,000 Light across FATEs, dungeons, trials, raids, and PvP.

Item Qty needed How to obtain Exchange cost
Farm 2,000 Light 1 Quest: 'Mmmmmm, Soulglazed Relics' grants a Zodiac Glass. Accumulate 2,000 Light from FATEs, dungeons, trials, raids, and PvP (8–128 points per completion, more during bonus windows), then return to Jalzahn. Light Farming

7. Zodiac Braves (i125)

Clear four ordered dungeon-treasure quests — 'Atma 2.0'.

Item Qty needed How to obtain Exchange cost
'A Ponze of Flesh' 1 First of four 'Atma 2.0' quests — collect dungeon-specific treasures instead of FATE Atma. Must be completed in order before the next quest unlocks. Dungeon Treasure
'Labor of Love' 1 Second of the four quests — more dungeon-specific treasures (Bombard Core, Sacred Spring Water, and similar). Dungeon Treasure
'Method in His Malice' 1 Third of the four quests — dungeon-specific treasures plus Soldiery currency. Dungeon Treasure / Soldiery
'A Treasured Mother' 1 Final of the four quests — completing it unlocks the Zodiac Braves weapon model (particle effects still locked until Zeta). Dungeon Treasure

8. Zodiac Zeta (i135)

Buy 12 Mahatma with Lesser Tomestones and Light, then claim your finished Zeta weapon.

Item Qty needed How to obtain Exchange cost
Mahatma 12 Bought with Lesser Tomestones once you've banked enough Light (Nexus's Light carries forward as 'Zeta resonance'). Total across all Mahatma: roughly 2,400 tomestones and 480 Light. 200 Tomestones
Zodiac Zeta Weapon 1 Turn in every bound Mahatma to Jalzahn to finish the reforge and claim your completed Zodiac Zeta — the final stage of the ARR relic weapon! Quest Complete
